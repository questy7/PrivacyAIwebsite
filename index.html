The process

From sensitive request to anonymized AI response

Five steps, every time — the only two that ever see identifying details are yours, and our own relay is sealed off from reading any of it.

01
You write normally
Redaction runs on your own device as you type, before anything is sent anywhere.
02
Identifying details get tokenized
Names, SSNs, emails, and other identifiers are swapped for placeholders, locally. The substance of your question is unaffected and still travels through.
03
Sent through our sealed relay
Our own systems forward it inside a hardware-isolated environment — even our staff and ordinary infrastructure can't read what's passing through.
04
Claude or GPT answers
The AI reasons over your real question — just without the real names or numbers attached to it.
05
Your device restores the details
The response comes back tokenized; only your device holds the map back to the real names.
Controls that support accountability

Accountability stays with your firm. We provide the controls and evidence to support it.

Your organization remains responsible to its clients, patients, professional bodies, and regulators. PrivacyAI is responsible for operating the safeguards it promises and helping your firm document how those safeguards were applied.

Local redaction
Identifying details are removed on your device before transmission.
Provider separation
The AI provider receives requests through PrivacyAI, not your firm's individual account.
No content logs
PrivacyAI does not retain message content or your local identity map.
Local audit record
Your firm keeps evidence of how AI was used and which safeguards were applied.
