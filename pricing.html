For firms with 6+ people

Volume pricing, by seat

Tokens pool across your whole team — a light user and a heavy user balance out naturally, no per-person caps to manage. Support-staff seats (paralegals, admin) are priced lower than attorney/CPA seats.

Seats Full seat (attorney / CPA) Light seat (paralegal / support) Example

For scale: ChatGPT Enterprise has a 150-seat minimum and starts around $45-75/seat. This pricing is built for the firms in between — too big for a solo tier, too small for an enterprise contract.

Why not usage-only pricing? Metered-only billing creates unpredictable monthly bills, which is exactly what professionals evaluating a new tool don't want. A flat tier with a real included allowance is easier to budget against.
Why not unlimited? Unlimited plans quietly rely on light users subsidizing heavy ones. We'd rather be upfront about what's included and let you add capacity if you need it, than build a plan that only works because most people don't use it fully.
